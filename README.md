# Brushflares

Hand-painted lens flares and streak bursts for Blender.

**This repository is the bug tracker and support page for Brushflares. The add-on itself is a paid product and its source is not hosted here.**

Buy it on [Gumroad](https://merlinperry.gumroad.com) or [Superhive](https://superhivemarket.com).

## Found a bug?

[Open an issue.](../../issues/new)

To get it fixed quickly, include:

- Your **Blender version** and OS
- Your **Brushflares version** (Edit > Preferences > Add-ons > Brushflares)
- Which **preset or Look** you were using, if it's relevant
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
- A screenshot or short screen recording if it's a visual problem
- A cut-down `.blend` that shows it, if you can

Crashes and errors: open the Blender system console (Window > Toggle System Console on Windows) and paste the traceback into the issue. That single step usually saves a round of questions.

## Feature requests

Also issues. Say what you're trying to achieve rather than the button you want, and there may be a way to do it already.
